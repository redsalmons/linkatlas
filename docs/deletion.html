Request deletion of your LinkAtlas data

LinkAtlas stores a device certificate identity, your lookup history, and purchase entitlements. To request deletion of this data:

Send an email from any address to:

poseidonyang888@gmail.com

With subject LinkAtlas Data Deletion Request. If you can, mention your device name or the approximate date you registered — it helps us locate the right record. We will confirm once the deletion is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your device certificate registration
  • Lookup history and associated audit records

Purchase records are kept as required by Google Play / Apple for billing and tax purposes.
