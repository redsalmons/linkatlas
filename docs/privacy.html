LinkAtlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

LinkAtlas ("the app") shows which domains and IP addresses your device communicates with and provides lookup and analysis for them. This policy describes what data the app and service handle.

Data collected by the app

  • Device identifier — a device certificate is issued at first launch and used for mutual-TLS authentication with our service.
  • Lookup requests — when you look up a domain or IP, the app sends that domain or IP address to our server (app.freedomsalmon.com).
  • Purchase records — subscription and one-time purchase receipts are verified through Google Play Billing or Apple in-app purchase. We store entitlement status, not payment card details.
  • Usage logs — the server keeps minimal audit records of lookups and API calls for abuse prevention and debugging.

Data that stays on your device

  • Traffic observation — the app's local capture of your network connections is processed on-device for the map and stats views. It is not uploaded.
  • Location — used only on-device to draw connection routes on the map. Location is never transmitted.

Third parties

  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — process purchases under their own policies.
  • AI analysis provider — when AI analysis is requested, the queried domain or IP is sent to our AI provider (via OpenRouter) to generate the summary. No account identifiers are included in that payload.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, or browsing history beyond what is needed for the lookup feature you invoke.

Retention and security

Query and audit records are retained for operational purposes. Transport uses mutual TLS with per-device certificates issued by a private CA. You may contact us to request deletion of your records.
